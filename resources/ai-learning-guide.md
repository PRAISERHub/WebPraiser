# A thoughtful start with AI learning

PRAISER introductory resource · Adapt this guide to your learning context.

## Before using AI
- State the concept or skill you want to understand.
- Try the task yourself and identify where you need help.
- Check your course's expectations for AI use.
- Avoid entering personal, confidential, or restricted information.

## While using AI
- Ask for explanations, examples, and questions that help you practice.
- Ask the tool to explain assumptions and identify uncertainty.
- Compare its response with course materials and reliable sources.
- Verify citations by locating and reading the original sources.

## After using AI
- Explain the concept in your own words without looking at the response.
- Solve a new example independently.
- Record what you verified, corrected, and still do not understand.
- Acknowledge AI assistance where your course requires it.

## Reflection prompt
What can I now explain or do independently? What will I practice next?
