# Make your AI workflow reproducible

PRAISER introductory checklist · Adapt for your discipline and methods.

## Model and environment
- Record provider, model identifier, version if available, and access date.
- Record configuration, parameters, dependencies, and runtime environment.
- Identify unavailable version details and external services that may change.

## Inputs and process
- Document data sources, permissions, selection, and preprocessing.
- Preserve prompts, system instructions, and relevant interaction context.
- Record tools, retrieval sources, and human interventions.
- Set and record random seeds where supported.

## Evaluation
- Define evaluation criteria before interpreting results.
- Record verification methods, baselines, errors, and exclusions.
- Distinguish generated outputs from verified conclusions.
- Report variability across runs and limits on exact reproduction.

## Sharing
- Include setup instructions and a minimal worked example.
- State licenses and conditions for data, code, and outputs separately.
- Remove secrets and information that cannot be shared.
- Document limitations and provide a stable version reference.
